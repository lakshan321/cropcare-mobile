## 1. Evaluation Objectives
The objective of this usability evaluation is to assess the usability, clarity, accessibility, and error recovery of the CropCare mobile application with **5 representative real or proxy users** (3 farmers / crop growers and 2 agricultural extension officers / agricultural science proxies).

The evaluation observes:
1. **Task Completion Rate (%):** Did the participant successfully complete the task without unrecoverable errors?
2. **Time-on-Task (Seconds):** Total duration from receiving instructions to achieving goal state.
3. **Error Frequency:** Count of navigation missteps, input validation rejections, or confused taps.
4. **Assistance Level (0–3 Scale):**
   - 0 = Independent completion without assistance.
   - 1 = Minor verbal prompt needed.
   - 2 = Moderate guidance required.
   - 3 = Task aborted or completed by facilitator.
5. **Single Ease Question (SEQ, 1–7):** "Overall, how easy or difficult was this task?" (1 = Very Difficult, 7 = Very Easy).
6. **System Usability Scale (SUS):** 10-item standard post-test questionnaire.

## 3. Evaluation Tasks

### Task 1: Authentication & Language Setup
- **Instruction:** "Open CropCare, switch the application language to your native preference, and log into your account using your credentials."
- **Success Criteria:** User successfully toggles language and authenticates to the role-appropriate dashboard.

### Task 2: Leaf Disease Photo Capture & Analysis (Member 1 Workload)
- **Instruction:** "You notice yellowing circular spots on your tomato plant leaves. Review the photo guidelines, capture or upload a leaf photo, inspect the preview, and submit it for health analysis."
- **Success Criteria:** User checks guidelines, captures leaf, inspects preview, and reaches the diagnosis outcome screen.

### Task 3: Review Treatment & Manage Care Tasks (Member 2 Workload)
- **Instruction:** "Inspect the symptoms and treatment recommendations for the identified disease. Add a field care task to spray organic neem oil on your plot, and mark it as completed."
- **Success Criteria:** User navigates to Treatment, adds a custom care task, and toggles the completion checkbox.

### Task 4: Submit Expert Consultation & Track Progress (Member 3 Workload)
- **Instruction:** "You want a second opinion from the local agricultural office. Submit an expert consultation request linking your diagnosis, description, and location. Then check your consultation status."
- **Success Criteria:** User submits consultation and verifies its appearance in the Cases screen.

### Task 5: Officer Case Queue, Private Draft & Response Publishing (Member 4 Workload)
- **Instruction:** "Log in as an Agricultural Officer. Locate the newly submitted high-priority tomato case, write a private draft, publish official treatment advice, and review the outbreak analytics summary."
- **Success Criteria:** Officer saves draft, publishes response, updates case status to Resolved, and inspects analytics breakdown.
